## 🎯 Quick Test Steps

1. **Open the app**
2. **Navigate to Settings** (from Dashboard or Menu)
3. **Scroll down to "⚙️ App Settings" section**
4. **Tap each option:**
   - ✅ Notification → Opens Notifications page
   - ✅ Privacy & Security → Opens Privacy page
   - ✅ Language → Opens Language selector
   - ✅ About → Opens About page
   - ✅ Help → Opens Help guide
5. **Use back button (←)** to return to Settings

---

## 💡 Tips

- **All pages have back buttons** in the top-left
- **Settings are saved automatically** when you toggle switches
- **Language changes** require app restart
- **Privacy settings** are stored locally
- **Help sections** are expandable (tap to open/close)
